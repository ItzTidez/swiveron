WHAT IT SWITCHES

Every profile can control

Set up as many profiles as you want. Each one can touch any combination of the below, skip what you don't need.

Apps

Launch programs, shortcuts, or URLs and close apps you don't want running all in one switch.

Audio

Switch both your default playback device and your microphone, independently per profile.

Monitors

Extend, clone, or drop to a single display automatically when you switch contexts.

Lighting

Push a color to your OpenRGB-controlled devices (keyboard, GPU, case fans) per profile.

Hotkeys

Every profile gets its own global hotkey, remappable any time, live, no restart needed.

Quiet mode

Flag a profile to signal it should stay quiet, without notifications. Useful for recording or focus sessions.
